The concept

A boutique gated community of only twenty-five residences, designed around privacy, wellbeing and timeless Mediterranean living.

Inspired by the golden era of the Israeli coastline, Solera combines contemporary architecture with warm materials, native landscaping and carefully curated shared spaces.

Every residence opens to a private outdoor room: a garden, a terrace, or a rooftop solarium facing the sea.

Three reasons to choose Solera 01
(1)

Real-life location

Set between quiet beaches, the Carmel ridge trails and the coastal train line. Thirty minutes to Haifa, forty to Tel Aviv, and two minutes barefoot to the sea.

(2)

A community, not a complex

Instead of corridors, garden paths connect the homes. Solera feels closer to a group of private houses than to an apartment building.

(3)

Built to keep its value

Natural stone, brass and lime plaster. Materials chosen to age well on a salt-air coastline, with full documentation open to every buyer.
