The Retreat

Kedem brings together one lodge and five stone suites on the crater's northern rim. For half a billion years this land has kept its own time; we built quietly, so it still does.

The Wilderness

We stand for balance, where people and desert thrive together

Rooted in partnership with the local Bedouin community, our ethos ensures that when our neighbours prosper, so does the wilderness. Ibex graze the ridge at dawn; we schedule around them, not the other way round.

Suites

Generous proportions, magnificent silence

Each suite opens directly onto the crater rim. Private plunge pools, indoor and outdoor showers, and spacious living areas keep the experience intentionally intimate: eleven keys in total, never more.
