What we deliberately do not do

Transparency up front saves disappointment later.

  • ✕No WordPress, no Wix. We build in clean code. That is what enables free-form design, speed, and zero security maintenance. If you specifically want Wix, we are not the right address.
  • ✕No full online store. Carts, inventory and shipping are a different world. Payments on the site? Yes, as an upgrade. A complete store? We will point you to the right solution.
  • ✕We never touch card details. Every payment runs through secure, regulated payment providers. Card details never pass through us and are never stored with us.
  • ✕No "first place on Google" promises. Whoever promises that is not telling the truth. We build the best foundations there are and work on ongoing presence.
  • ✕We do not keep your passwords. Connections to your accounts are done securely, without a password ever passing through chat or email.
  • ✕No official accessibility certification. We build with good accessibility practices. Formal certification, where required, is done with a licensed accessibility consultant.
Why clean code? A static site has no database to hack, no plugins to update, and no "the site is down". It loads fast, it is secure by its very structure, and the files belong to you.
